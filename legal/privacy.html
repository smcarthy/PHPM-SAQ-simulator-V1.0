Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to PHPM Leadership Practice and the PHPM Exam Hub, maintained by Stephen McCarthy. They are independent educational tools and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada.

Plugin and ChatGPT use

The developer does not operate a separate server or API that receives plugin conversations, uploaded files, or generated responses. Your use of ChatGPT and the plugin is processed by OpenAI and is subject to the privacy terms and account controls that OpenAI makes available to you. Do not enter identifiable patient information, confidential Royal College examination material, or private resident submissions.

PHPM Exam Hub website

The website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not require an account and does not use developer-operated analytics. Written-practice responses, timer state, selections, and scores may be stored in your browser's local storage so you can resume your work. That information remains on your device unless you choose to download or share it. You can clear it using the site's reset controls or your browser settings.

GitHub Pages and other resources requested by the site, such as hosted web fonts, may process ordinary connection information including IP address, browser details, and request timestamps under their own privacy terms.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and attachments. It is used to respond, investigate problems, maintain safety, and improve the educational resource. Access is limited and information is retained only as reasonably needed for those purposes or legal obligations. You may request deletion of a support message, subject to those obligations.

Data sales and advertising

The developer does not sell personal information and does not use personal information from the plugin or website for targeted advertising.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to smcca104@uottawa.ca.
