Support

This page provides support for PHPM Leadership Practice and the PHPM Exam Hub. The service is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada.

Contact

Email smcca104@uottawa.ca for access problems, technical issues, content corrections, or other support. Support is provided on a best-effort basis without a guaranteed response time.

What to include

  • The product name and feature you were using.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • The date, browser or ChatGPT client, and a screenshot if useful.

Do not send identifiable patient information, confidential examination material, passwords, access tokens, or private resident submissions.

Educational-content concerns

AI-generated questions, feedback, scores, and model answers are provisional educational material. Report suspected errors through the support email. Current official guidance and verified expert review take precedence.
